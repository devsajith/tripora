# Kerala Travel Agency Design System (Tripora Editorial Journey)

## Brand & Style

This design system embodies the warmth, serenity, and bespoke craftsmanship of slow luxury travel. Rooted in the backwaters, mist-draped tea estates, and heritage coasts of Kerala and pan-India journeys, the aesthetic marries high-end travel journalism with effortless, contemporary digital utility.

### Brand Personality & Emotional Tone
- **Atmospheric & Calming:** Evoking quiet mornings on a cedar houseboat, monsoon-dappled foliage, and warm earthen clay.
- **Editorial Elegance:** Layouts prioritize full-bleed photography, considered white space, and graceful typographic hierarchy over high-density dashboard noise.
- **Rooted Authenticity:** Leans into organic forest tones, tactile micro-details, and authentic narratives.

### Design Movement
**Organic Editorial Modernism:** A fusion of high-fashion editorial print architecture (high-contrast serifs, expansive margins, asymmetric story containers) and warm organic minimalism (supple radii, subtle atmospheric shadows, tinted natural surfaces).

## Colors

- **Primary (`#00261D` / `#123C32` — Deep Forest Green):** The foundational anchor for headers, brand marks, primary CTA fills, and primary framing containers.
- **Secondary (`#E8DDC8` / `#655E4D` — Warm Sand):** The softening agent for badges, card highlight fills, and decorative dividers.
- **Tertiary / Accent (`#C86B4A` — Terracotta):** Selective high-intent conversion moments ("Reserve Journey", "Check Dates"), active states, and focal tags.
- **Neutral Core (`#151D1A` — Charcoal Text):** Deep off-black with subtle green-slate undertones.
- **Canvas (`#F2FCF5` — Soft Sage White):** Natural rice-paper / cardamom off-white.

## Typography

- **Headlines:** Playfair Display
